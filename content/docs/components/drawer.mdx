## Examples

### Side drawer

Set `position` to `"left"` or `"right"` to slide the drawer in from the side. The
`swipeDirection` is derived from the position automatically.

### Inset

Use `variant="inset"` on `DrawerContent` to float the drawer away from the screen edges
with rounded corners on all sides.

### Nested

Drawers can be nested by rendering a `Drawer` inside another drawer's content. The parent
drawer scales back and peeks behind the child while it is open.
