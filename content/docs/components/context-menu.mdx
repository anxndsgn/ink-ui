## Examples

### With addons

Add icons, text, or other supplementary content with `ContextMenuItemAddon`. Place it before
`ContextMenuItemLabel` for a leading addon or after the label for a trailing addon.

### Checkbox items

Use `ContextMenuCheckboxItem` for toggleable options.

> **Note:** The checkbox indicator occupies the leading addon column. Avoid adding a leading
> `ContextMenuItemAddon`; place any supplementary content after `ContextMenuItemLabel` instead.

### Radio items

> `ContextMenuItemAddon`; place any supplementary content after `ContextMenuItemLabel` instead.
